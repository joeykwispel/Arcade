Bug Bash, a tower defense game drawn on a canvas. On the level select, choose a level with the arrow keys and press Enter. While playing: keys 1 to 6 choose a tool, the arrow keys move a cursor over the map, Enter installs the tool or selects the one under the cursor, U upgrades, S sells, Space starts the next sprint, F toggles double speed and Escape pauses.

This game needs WebAssembly, which this browser doesn't allow.
